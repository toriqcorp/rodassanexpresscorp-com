About Us

Built on trust, run like family.

RODAS & SAN EXPRESS CORP is a family-run interstate freight carrier based in Elgin, Illinois. Founded and operated by Darinel Rodas, we built this company on a simple idea: treat every driver like family and every load like it's our own.

As a small carrier, we keep our fleet lean and our standards high — late-model equipment, consistent no-touch dry van freight, and an open line to dispatch and ownership whenever a driver needs it. We're proud to be a growing part of the American trucking industry, hauling freight across the lower 48 while staying true to the hands-on, relationship-first way we started.
